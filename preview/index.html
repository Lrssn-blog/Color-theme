Lrssn colour themes

Hearth and Shore

Two colour themes built around the Lrssn orange, #fc6600. Hearth is a cottage on New Year's Eve, warm by the fire while fireworks go off in the cold outside. Shore is a summer evening on a beach, with a bonfire, clear tropical water and coral.

In the editor and the terminal

The same file and the same shell in both themes.

Dark · lrssn-hearth

Lrssn Hearth

Smoked timber and candlelight. Orange, gold and ember from the fire, frost blue from the cold outside, and pink, violet and green from the fireworks.

Light · lrssn-shore

Lrssn Shore

Warm sand and the deep water of the lagoon. Orange from the bonfire, blue and teal from the water, and pink, red and purple from the coral.

The palettes

Every role of both palettes. The number under a text colour is its contrast ratio on the editor background, and every one of them is at least 4.5.

On the website

The website's tokens from website/_theme.scss: Shore is the light theme and Hearth the dark one.

Where to find them

Each file is generated from palette/hearth.toml and palette/shore.toml. The README has the install steps.

VS Code
An extension in vscode/, with both themes.
Neovim
:colorscheme lrssn-hearth or lrssn-shore, from colors/ and lua/.
Terminals
Alacritty, Ghostty, kitty, Windows Terminal and iTerm2, in terminal/.
Omarchy
A theme folder each, with wallpapers, in omarchy/.
Homarr
One stylesheet for both themes, which follows the colour scheme, in homarr/.
The website
The two maps of the stylesheet, in website/_theme.scss.
LaTeX
The package lrssntheme, with the options shore and hearth, in latex/.
